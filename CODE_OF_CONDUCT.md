# Code of Conduct

ParamMesh is an open-source project for learning, building, and experimenting with distributed training. Everyone participating in its repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions is expected to help keep the community respectful and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming to contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Discuss technical ideas and disagreements respectfully, with evidence where possible.
- Give actionable feedback; assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy, credentials, and confidential information.
- Credit other people's work and respect project licenses.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, and sustained disruptive conduct are not permitted.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer using GitHub's contact options where available. If private contact is unavailable, avoid publishing sensitive personal details in a public issue. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close discussions, restrict participation, or ban repeat offenders. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

This code of conduct applies to all project-managed community spaces and to conduct representing the project.
